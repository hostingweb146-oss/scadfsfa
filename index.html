Inspection Guide

Hidden cameras rely on lenses that curve to capture light. These lenses often reflect light back as a tiny, bright dot.

  • Step 1: Dim the lights in the room as much as possible.
  • Step 2: Start scanning and turn on the flashlight (if supported).
  • Step 3: Slowly pan the camera across suspicious items: smoke detectors, clocks, mirrors, vents, and plush toys.
  • Step 4: Look for sharp, bright pinpoints of light blinking or reflecting back on the screen.
Disclaimer: This tool highlights bright spots and reflections locally in your browser. It cannot definitively prove the existence or absence of a hidden camera. False positives (mirrors, glass, dust) will occur. Do not rely solely on this app for your physical security.
